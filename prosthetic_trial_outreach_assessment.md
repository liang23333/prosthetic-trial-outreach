## 2. In-Depth Profile of 10 Current Lower-Limb Prosthetic Trials

### Study 1: LIMBER UniLeg 3D-Printed Prosthetic Limbs
* **NCT Number**: NCT06648798
* **Lead Sponsor**: LIMBER Prosthetics & Orthotics Inc (Collaborators: UC San Diego, NIH NICHD)
* **Overall Status**: RECRUITING (Completion: Oct 2026)
* **Phase / Study Design**: Phase NA | Interventional single-group crossover (*N* = 30)
* **Objective**: Evaluate whether a rapidly manufactured, low-cost unibody 3D-printed transtibial prosthetic leg matches traditional passive limbs.
* **Eligible Criteria**: Age 18–75, unilateral transtibial amputee >1 year, normal sensation.
* **Gaps & Exclusions**:
  - **Explicitly excludes diabetic amputees** ("Are not diabetic (self reported)").
  - Requires walking ≥14,000 steps/week (~1 mile/day), filtering out low-mobility users.
  - Restricted to residents within 50 miles of UCSD.

### Study 2: Magnetic Bead Tracking & e-OPRA Bionic Limb
* **NCT Number**: NCT06391697
* **Lead Sponsor**: Brigham and Women's Hospital (Collaborators: MIT Media Lab, Integrum)
* **Overall Status**: ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING (Completion: May 2027)
* **Phase / Study Design**: Phase NA | Interventional Device Feasibility (*N* = 7)
* **Objective**: Implant pairs of magnetic beads in residual muscle paired with the e-OPRA bone-anchored skeletal interface for real-time neural bionic control.
* **Eligible Criteria**: Age 22–65, unilateral or bilateral transtibial amputees.
* **Gaps & Exclusions**:
  - Requires **K3 or above** mobility status (high-cadence community ambulator).
  - Excludes diabetes, severe peripheral vascular disease (PVD), neuropathy, BMI > 40, and smokers.
  - Requires prior consultation with two separate limb-salvage surgeons.

### Study 3: Neural-Controlled Powered Prosthesis Across Real-World Tasks
* **NCT Number**: NCT07204912
* **Lead Sponsor**: Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)
* **Overall Status**: RECRUITING (Completion: May 2028)
* **Phase / Study Design**: Phase NA | Interventional Basic Science (*N* = 10)
* **Objective**: Assess embodiment, stability, and biomechanics of a neural-controlled powered bionic knee or ankle during complex everyday movements.
* **Eligible Criteria**: Age 18–70, unilateral transfemoral amputation, adequate socket fit.
* **Gaps & Exclusions**:
  - Strictly requires **K3 or above** functional mobility.
  - Excludes patients with BMI > 40, psychiatric illness, or severe systemic comorbidity.
  - Tiny sample size (*N* = 10) concentrated at MIT campus.

### Study 4: Frontal Plane Adaptability in Novel Foot Prosthesis
* **NCT Number**: NCT06214026
* **Lead Sponsor**: University of Washington (Collaborator: US Department of Defense)
* **Overall Status**: RECRUITING (Completion: April 2027)
* **Phase / Study Design**: Phase NA | Interventional Crossover (*N* = 96)
* **Objective**: Test dynamic carbon fiber foot with locked vs unlocked frontal plane adaptable linkage in above-knee and bilateral amputees.
* **Eligible Criteria**: Age ≥16, unilateral or bilateral amputation, community dwelling.
* **Gaps & Exclusions**:
  - Must walk >400 meters on level ground without pain.
  - **Explicitly requires English literacy**: "People must have the ability to read, write, and comprehend English." Excludes non-English speaking immigrants.
  - Excludes patients relying on wheelchairs.

### Study 5: Improving Socket Fit in Female and Male Veterans (SocketFit)
* **NCT Number**: NCT05989243
* **Lead Sponsor**: VA Office of Research and Development
* **Overall Status**: RECRUITING (Completion: March 2027)
* **Phase / Study Design**: Phase NA | Observational Cohort (*N* = 40)
* **Objective**: Compare adjustable panel prosthetic sockets with standard sockets to optimize comfort and reduce socket pistoning.
* **Eligible Criteria**: US Veterans aged 18–65, unilateral transtibial (20) or transfemoral (20).
* **Gaps & Positive Highlights**:
  - **Positive**: Mandates equal 50% female / 50% male enrollment (10 men, 10 women per group) to address female amputee underrepresentation.
  - Limited strictly to military Veterans receiving care at Rocky Mountain VA.
  - Excludes users requiring an assistive device for the intact leg.

### Study 6: Transtibial Osseointegration Surgery Study (TOSS)
* **NCT Number**: NCT06636136
* **Lead Sponsor**: Walter Reed National Military Medical Center (Collaborator: Penn Medicine)
* **Overall Status**: RECRUITING (Completion: April 2030)
* **Phase / Study Design**: Phase NA | Prospective Cohort FDA Pivotal Study (*N* = 30)
* **Objective**: Evaluate 5-year soft tissue complications, bone remodeling, and biomechanics of the OPRA bone-anchored fixture in transtibial amputees.
* **Eligible Criteria**: Military health beneficiaries aged 22–65 with socket intolerance.
* **Gaps & Exclusions**:
  - Military DEERS eligibility mandatory at primary site.
  - Excludes diabetes with complications, severe PVD, neuropathy, osteoporosis, and BMI > 35.

### Study 7: Microprocessor Knee Utilization in a K2 Population (ASCENT K2)
* **NCT Number**: NCT04784429
* **Lead Sponsor**: Otto Bock Healthcare (Collaborator: Hanger Clinic)
* **Overall Status**: ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING (Completion: Dec 2026)
* **Phase / Study Design**: Phase NA | Randomized Controlled Trial (*N* = 107)
* **Objective**: Measure fall reduction, health-related quality of life, and morbidity changes from microprocessor knees (MPKs) in limited community ambulators.
* **Eligible Criteria**: Age ≥65 years, unilateral transfemoral amputation, K2 ambulator status.
* **Gaps & Positive Highlights**:
  - **Exemplary inclusion**: Specifically targets older adults (≥65) and K2 lower-mobility users.
  - Accommodates English and Spanish speaking participants across multi-center clinics.
  - Excludes weight ≥275 lbs and frequent socket replacement history.

### Study 8: Criteria for Advanced Prosthetic Foot Prescription
* **NCT Number**: NCT03505983
* **Lead Sponsor**: VA New York Harbor Healthcare System (Collaborators: Walter Reed, James A. Haley VA, VA Puget Sound)
* **Overall Status**: ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING (Completion: Sept 2026)
* **Phase / Study Design**: Phase NA | Interventional Crossover (*N* = 91)
* **Objective**: Establish clinical prescription guidelines for articulating vs non-articulating Energy Storing and Returning (ESR) feet.
* **Eligible Criteria**: Veterans, service members, and civilians with unilateral transtibial limb loss.
* **Gaps & Exclusions**:
  - Excludes body weight >275 lbs.
  - Excludes medical conditions with rapid residual limb volume shifts (e.g., end-stage renal disease on hemodialysis).

### Study 9: Transfemoral Osseointegration Functional Assessment (OSTEO-OUTCOME)
* **NCT Number**: NCT06737770
* **Lead Sponsor**: Istituto Ortopedico Rizzoli (Bologna, Italy)
* **Overall Status**: RECRUITING (Completion: Jan 2027)
* **Phase / Study Design**: Phase NA | Interventional Single Group (*N* = 15)
* **Objective**: Assess gait kinetics, osteoperception, and comfort of direct skeletal fixation for transfemoral amputees suffering socket complications.
* **Eligible Criteria**: Age 18–65, transfemoral amputation due to trauma, tumor, or infection.
* **Gaps & Exclusions**:
  - Strictly excludes vascular disease, uncontrolled diabetes, and osteoporosis.
  - Age ceiling at 65 excludes senior citizens.

### Study 10: Locomotor Adaptations for Stairs and Slopes (JCE)
* **NCT Number**: NCT04030650
* **Lead Sponsor**: Centre Hospitalier Universitaire Dijon Bourgogne (France)
* **Overall Status**: RECRUITING (Completion: May 2028)
* **Phase / Study Design**: Phase NA | Observational Machine Learning (*N* = 70)
* **Objective**: Develop predictive sensor algorithms for microprocessor prostheses to autonomously anticipate stairs and terrain inclines.
* **Eligible Criteria**: Adults with major lower limb amputation using prosthesis for ≥3 months.
* **Gaps & Exclusions**:
  - Requires public health insurance coverage.
  - Excludes any concomitant gait-impairing conditions (osteoarthritis of contralateral leg, spinal stenosis).

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Diversity Enrollment Target**: ≥35% racial minority representation (matching Cook County demographic amputation burden).
* **Etiology Equity**: ≥40% dysvascular/diabetic representation in observational/feasibility trials.
* **Mobility Inclusivity**: ≥25% enrollment of K1/K2 classified amputees.
* **Gender Equity**: ≥35% female amputee participation.
* **Language Access**: 100% availability of bilingual Spanish/English study navigators and translated study forms.
